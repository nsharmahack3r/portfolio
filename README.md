## 👨‍💻 About Me

Full-stack engineer with 4+ years of industry experience across mobile, web, backend, and AI systems. I build things end-to-end — from real-time Flutter apps and scalable Node.js pipelines to computer vision models running on embedded edge devices.
On the AI side, I train and deploy computer vision pipelines (YOLO, segmentation, OpenCV), work with LLM integration and RAG architectures, and have published research on Edge AI systems for UAV imagery — running inference directly on hardware in the field, no cloud required.
On the product side, I've shipped 5+ apps to iOS and Android, architected real-time WebSocket systems serving thousands of concurrent users, and led teams of 30+ developers.

- 🚀 **5+ apps** shipped to iOS & Android
